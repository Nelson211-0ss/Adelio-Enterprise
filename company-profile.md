## Executive Summary

Adelio & Sons Enterprise is a multi-sector enterprise headquartered in Magwi Town, South Sudan, delivering **IT services and equipment, stationery, office equipment, agricultural supplies, branding and design, and business consultancy** through a single point of contact.

Rather than asking clients to coordinate an IT vendor, a stationery supplier, a print shop, and a consultant separately, Adelio & Sons consolidates all six functions under one accountable partner — reducing procurement overhead and giving corporate offices, schools, farms, retailers, NGOs, and government agencies one number to call for nearly everything that keeps an operation running.

We have built particularly deep experience supplying and supporting international humanitarian and development organizations operating in South Sudan — see [Clients & Partners](#clients--partners) for a full list.

This document outlines who we are, what we offer, how we work, and how to reach us.

## About Us

Adelio & Sons Enterprise began as a small IT support outfit and has since grown into a full-service enterprise spanning six complementary business lines. That growth was deliberate: clients who trusted us with their networks and hardware began asking whether we could also handle their office supplies, then their branding, then general business advice — and rather than referring that work elsewhere, we built the capability in-house.

Today, our team of certified technicians, supply specialists, and consultants serves businesses of every size, guided by a simple operating principle: **show up, deliver on time, and stand behind the work.**

## What We Do

Adelio & Sons Enterprise operates across six complementary business lines. Clients can engage us for a single line or bundle several together under one account.

### 1. IT Services & Equipment
End-to-end technology support for businesses that can't afford downtime.
- Network setup, configuration, and ongoing management
- Hardware and software procurement, repair, and support
- Cybersecurity services and threat protection
- Cloud migration and data backup solutions
- IT equipment supply (computers, servers, peripherals)

### 2. Stationery Supplies
Everyday office consumables, sourced reliably and delivered on schedule.
- Pens, paper, and general writing supplies
- Filing, folders, and desk essentials
- Recurring or one-off bulk restocking

### 3. Office Equipment
The hardware a workplace runs on, beyond computers.
- Printers, scanners, and multifunction devices
- Office furniture and workstation equipment
- General workplace equipment sourcing

### 4. Agricultural Supplies
Support for the farms and agribusinesses in our operating region.
- Farm tools and general equipment
- Seeds and agro-inputs (fertilizers, crop-care products)
- Irrigation equipment and water supply materials

### 5. Branding & Design
Visual identity and materials that make a business look as professional as it operates.
- Logo and corporate identity design
- Banners, signage, and branded displays
- Promotional merchandise and corporate gifts
- Marketing collateral and custom branded printing

### 6. Consultancy Services
Independent advice for businesses deciding what to do next.
- Technology and digital strategy
- Procurement advisory across IT, office, and agricultural supply
- Business process and operations consultancy
- Vendor and license management
